ARC-24 Strong

Store link destinations as identity, not position

Copies and internal links are both shipped features, and today they are unreconciled: page order changes after the destination index is written, so a copied report re-aims its links. Open the card.

Pair with the other P0, ARC-25, and the image input cluster (ARC-26, ARC-27, ARC-28).
