Devil's-advocate conclusion

Completed in this wave

Incompatible stdout sinks are rejected, ordinary documents use the generic renderer, and terminal/API contracts are restored with regression coverage.

Do not overreact

Do not introduce global caches, an aggregate resource budget, or a full concurrent PDF writer from static suspicion. The new 1/10/500-page, deep-chrome, forced-break, and cold-glyph baselines identify what should be measured next.

The optimization idea behind page islands is now restricted to an explicitly owned benchmark request with a parent-consistent tree and parity tests; it is no longer selected by ordinary document prose.
